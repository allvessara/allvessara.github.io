About Sara

A little about the person behind the process.

"I've lived the move I now help other people make."

Almost five years ago, I lived firsthand what I now help other people go through: I packed my bags and moved to Portugal.

Since then, a lot has changed — not just where I live, but how I see the world, other people, and the many different ways there are to live a life. Leaving my home country broadened my frame of reference, taught me to navigate bureaucracy, deadlines and uncertainty, and deepened a curiosity I've always had for stories, cultures and systems.

Maybe that's why I found in immigration work a space that fits me so well. I like understanding each person's context, what they need, what worries them, and what makes them trust a process that can often feel overwhelming.

Outside of work, I'm driven by that same curiosity. I love working out, traveling, discovering new places, a good brunch and quality time with the people I love. I have two dogs who take up a considerable part of my heart and my routine. I now live in Nazaré, home of the famous giant waves — and whenever I can, I'm chasing a sunset on the beach.

In the end, I think my work and my life meet in the same place: curiosity about people, about processes, and about the ways different systems and cultures connect.
