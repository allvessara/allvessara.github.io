Overview

Roots Global is a Portugal-based immigration consultancy serving English-speaking clients from the US, UK, Canada and Australia. As the company grew, the client experience began to show cracks: onboarding quality varied depending on who was handling the case, communication was reactive rather than proactive, and there was no system in place to guarantee the same standard of service for everyone.

I took on the design and implementation of a client experience framework from scratch — covering the entire post-sale journey, from first payment to visa approval.

The Challenge

The core problem was inconsistency. Internal practices existed, but they were undocumented, person-dependent, and varied significantly across visa types (D7, D8, D2, D1, Article 15). This created a series of downstream effects:

  • Clients received different levels of communication depending on who was handling their case
  • The team had no shared reference for what "good" onboarding looked like
  • There was no visibility into where each client stood in their journey
  • Reactive communication created unnecessary client anxiety and increased support volume
  • Case tracking lived in a dense, hard-to-navigate spreadsheet, making it difficult to see a client's stage, pending items and next touchpoints at a glance
The Solution

I designed a journey made up of 4 milestones and 8 touchpoints, standardizing the experience across all visa types while still allowing for specific variations where needed. The framework was documented, turned into templates, and rebuilt as a relational tracker in Notion for operational use — replacing the spreadsheet with a system that shows, in real time, each client's stage, what's pending, and what the next contact should be.

Overall daily communication volume dropped roughly in half, reflecting fewer "what's the status?" and clarification e-mails as the new structure took hold.

  • Standardized onboarding across all visa types — quality no longer depended on who handled the case
  • Less ad-hoc internal communication, with clear ownership and documented timing
  • A shared operational language for talking about client stages and progress
  • A replicable foundation that keeps working as the team grows
  • Reduced client anxiety, thanks to proactive communication at every stage
What I Learned
Proactive beats reactive

This project reinforced one thing: the highest-impact CX work happens before the client ever sends that first message. When communication is proactive and structured, clients feel secure — and support volume drops. The challenge at a small consultancy is building systems rigorous enough to scale, but simple enough for a growing team to actually use. The Roots framework was designed with that balance in mind: clear enough to follow without training, flexible enough to accommodate the particularities of each visa type.
